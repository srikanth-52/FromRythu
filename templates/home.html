Fair & Direct Model

How FromRythu Works

A transparent 4-step direct lifecycle from farm to your kitchen table.

1
Farmer Lists Harvest

Farmer logs into Farmer Portal, registers fresh crop yield, stock quantity, and sets direct fair price.

2
Customer Orders

Customer selects authentic farm produce with transparent harvest details and checks out with UPI or Cash on Delivery.

3
Fresh Pluck & Pack

Farmer receives order alert, harvests the batch freshly from field, packs hygienically, and updates tracking status.

4
Direct Delivery & Payment

Fresh produce is delivered right to consumer's doorstep. Payment goes directly into the farmer's bank or UPI!
